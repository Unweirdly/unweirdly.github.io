Race Notes

  • Fairy not allowed by default
  • Modified version (flight at higher levels) possible case-by-case
  • Creative concepts very welcome — strong story = negotiable

Restricted Spells (banned by default)

  • Wish
  • Silvery Barbs
  • Synaptic Static (or similar no-save control effects)
  • Simulacrum

Access may be unlocked later via rare quests / story in some economy modes.

12. Economy & Loot Style

A – Crafting & Business (classic Eberron)

Trade, contracts, Houses, Bastions → build influence & gear. Restricted spells do not exist here.

B – Market Economy

Big cities = magic markets. Gold buys items. Restricted spells = very rare loot only.

C – Story / Milestone Rewards

Items via quests, discoveries, epic moments. Restricted spells & legendaries possible through arcs.
